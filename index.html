How it works

Three steps. Zero couch.

  1. Drop it

    Pick any spot on the map. Write something only your friends will get.

  2. Go out

    Run, walk, ride or paddle. Your phone stays in your pocket.

  3. Find it

    Get close and it buzzes open. Not one step sooner.
